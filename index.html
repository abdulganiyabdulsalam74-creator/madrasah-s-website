WELCOME TO OUR SERVICES SECTION

In our madrasah Al-Abroor we do different kind of activities like:
  1. WEEKEND MADRASAH
    Saturdays and Sundays from 8am till 3pm in the afternoon
  2. WEEKDAYS MADRASAH (Monday-Thursday) For teenagers and adults especialy From 9am in the morning till dhuhr time

    On Fridays the Halqoh we do is on taoheed while during weekends the do fiqh
  5. Last but not the least: Our madrasah holds seminar to teach it's students our attendee different acts of Islam and how to behave in the society as Muslim brothers
